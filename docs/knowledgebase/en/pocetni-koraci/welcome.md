# Welcome to mojPoslić

Welcome to mojPoslić - the fast and secure platform for small jobs in Bosnia and Herzegovina! 🎉

## What is mojPoslić?

mojPoslić is a platform that connects people who need help with small jobs to those who can provide these services. Whether you need:

- 🔧 Handyman services
- 🧹 House cleaning
- 📦 Delivery
- 🌱 Garden services
- 💻 IT help
- 🎨 Creative services

...or any other short task, on mojPoslić you can quickly find or offer help.

## How does it work?

### For clients:
1. **Post a job** - Describe what you need
2. **Get offers** - Taskers will apply for your job
3. **Choose a tasker** - Select the best offer
4. **Complete the job** - Communicate and finish the task
5. **Rate** - Leave a review

### For taskers:
1. **Find jobs** - Browse available tasks
2. **Apply** - Send your proposal
3. **Get hired** - When client chooses you
4. **Complete task** - Professionally execute the job
5. **Get paid** - Receive payment and rating
