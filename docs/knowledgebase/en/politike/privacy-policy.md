# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 29, 2025  
**Website:** https://mojposlic.com  
**Platform name:** mojPoslić

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mojPoslić platform collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data.
By using our website and application, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

## 2. What data we collect

We collect data that you provide directly to us, as well as technical data about your use of the platform.
This includes:

- First and last name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password (securely stored and encrypted via Supabase authentication)
- Location (if approved, for displaying local advertisements)
- Technical data: IP address, device type, browser, operating system
- Cookies and similar technologies for functionality and analytics

## 3. How we use your data

We use data exclusively for:

- User registration and authentication via Supabase
- Publishing and managing job advertisements
- Connecting employers and service providers
- Sending notifications about platform activities (e.g., job applications, messages, etc.)
- Improving functionality, security, and user experience
- Usage analysis via cookies (e.g., Google Analytics)

## 4. Cookies

We use cookies for proper website functioning and analytics.
Users can change cookie settings in their browser at any time.
Types of cookies we use:

- **Essential cookies:** enable basic login and session functions.
- **Analytics cookies:** help us understand how users use the platform.
- **Functional cookies:** remember your settings and preferences.

## 5. Data sharing with third parties

Your data is not sold or transferred to third parties, except in the following cases:

- When necessary for platform functioning (e.g., Supabase for authentication and database).
- When required by law or competent authority.
- When the user decides to share information through public advertisements.

All partners we work with have built-in security measures in accordance with GDPR standards.

## 6. Data security

We use modern security measures, including encryption and SSL, to protect your data.
Access to personal data is limited only to authorized persons who use it exclusively for purposes stated in this policy.

## 7. Data retention period

Your data is kept as long as necessary to provide services or until you request deletion of your account.
Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed from the database.

## 8. Your rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have the right to:

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent

To exercise your rights, you can contact us via email:  
📧 [info@mojposlic.com](mailto:info@mojposlic.com)

## 9. Privacy policy changes

We reserve the right to change this policy.
All changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
We recommend that you periodically review the policy to stay informed about changes.

## 10. Contact

For all privacy-related questions, contact us via:  
📧 [info@mojposlic.com](mailto:info@mojposlic.com)
