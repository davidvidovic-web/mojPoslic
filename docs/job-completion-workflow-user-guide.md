# Job Completion Workflow - User Guide

**Version**: 1.0  
**Date**: July 22, 2025  
**Audience**: Taskers, Clients, End Users

## Overview

The Job Completion Workflow allows taskers and clients to formally complete jobs through a structured process. This ensures both parties are satisfied with the work before considering the job finished.

## How It Works

### The 4-Step Process

1. **Job Assignment** - Client accepts a tasker for their job
2. **Work in Progress** - Tasker begins and completes the work
3. **Mark Complete** - Tasker marks the work as finished
4. **Confirm Complete** - Client reviews and confirms completion

Once both steps 3 and 4 are complete, the job is finished and both parties can rate each other.

## For Taskers

### Step 1: View Your Assigned Jobs

1. Log into your dashboard
2. Navigate to "My Jobs" or "Active Assignments"
3. You'll see jobs where you've been selected by clients
4. Look for jobs with status "Work in Progress" or "Accepted"

### Step 2: Complete Your Work

- Work on the job according to the agreed requirements
- Communicate with the client if you have questions
- Ensure all deliverables are ready before marking complete

### Step 3: Mark Work as Complete

When you've finished the work:

1. **Find the job** in your dashboard
2. **Click "Mark Complete"** button
3. **Add completion notes** (optional but recommended):
   - Describe what you accomplished
   - Mention any additional work done
   - Provide instructions if needed
   
   Example:
   ```
   "I've completed the website redesign with all requested features:
   - Responsive design for mobile and desktop
   - Contact form with email integration  
   - SEO optimization completed
   - All content migrated from old site
   
   The site is live and ready for your review!"
   ```

4. **Click "Submit"**
5. You'll see the status change to "Work Completed (Awaiting Confirmation)"

### Step 4: Wait for Client Confirmation

- The client will receive a notification to review your work
- They have time to test and review everything
- You'll get notified when they confirm completion
- Once confirmed, you can rate your experience with the client

### What Happens Next

✅ **Job Status**: Changes to "Completed"  
✅ **Payment**: May be released (depending on payment terms)  
✅ **Rating**: You can now rate the client  
✅ **Portfolio**: Job can be added to your portfolio  

## For Clients

### Step 1: Monitor Job Progress

1. Check your dashboard regularly for job updates
2. Communicate with your assigned tasker as needed
3. You'll receive a notification when work is marked complete

### Step 2: Review Completed Work

When you receive a "Work Completed" notification:

1. **Review all deliverables** thoroughly
2. **Test functionality** if applicable
3. **Check against original requirements**
4. **Ask questions** if anything is unclear

### Step 3: Confirm Completion

If you're satisfied with the work:

1. **Go to your dashboard** and find the job
2. **Click "Confirm Complete"** button
3. **Add feedback** (optional but appreciated):
   - What you liked about the work
   - Quality assessment
   - Any standout efforts
   
   Example:
   ```
   "Excellent work! The website exceeds my expectations:
   - Design is modern and professional
   - All features work perfectly
   - Delivered on time
   - Great communication throughout
   
   Highly recommend this tasker!"
   ```

4. **Click "Confirm"**
5. The job status changes to "Completed"

### What If You're Not Satisfied?

If the work doesn't meet your expectations:

1. **Don't confirm completion yet**
2. **Message the tasker** with specific feedback
3. **Request revisions** if needed
4. **Work together** to resolve any issues
5. **Confirm only when satisfied**

### What Happens Next

✅ **Job Status**: Changes to "Completed"  
✅ **Payment**: Process payment according to agreed terms  
✅ **Rating**: You can now rate the tasker  
✅ **Archive**: Job moves to completed projects

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"I can't find the Mark Complete button"**
- Make sure you're the assigned tasker for this job
- Check that the job status is "Accepted" or "In Progress"
- Refresh your browser and try again

**"I marked it complete but the client hasn't responded"**
- Clients have time to review (usually 7 days)
- You can send a polite message asking for an update
- Contact support if no response after the review period

**"I want to make changes after marking complete"**
- You can still communicate with the client
- Make any requested revisions
- The client can confirm once they're satisfied

**"The Confirm button isn't working"**
- Make sure you're the job poster (client)
- Check that the tasker has marked work as complete
- Try refreshing your browser

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the client have to confirm completion?**  
A: Typically 7 days, but this may vary based on job complexity.

**Q: Can I rate someone before the job is fully completed?**  
A: No, ratings are only available after both parties confirm completion.

**Q: What if we disagree about whether work is complete?**  
A: Use the messaging system to discuss. If needed, contact support for mediation.

**Q: Can I edit my completion notes after submitting?**  
A: No, but you can send additional information through messages.

**Q: Is there a penalty for not confirming completion promptly?**  
A: No penalty, but it's courteous to review work promptly.

**Q: What happens if a client never confirms completion?**  
A: After the review period, the system may auto-confirm or escalate to support.
